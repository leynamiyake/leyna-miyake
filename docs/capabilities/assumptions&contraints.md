Rate Basis: All interest calculations, accruals, and discounting operate strictly on an actual-over-360 (ACT/360) day-count convention.

Transaction-Cost Treatment: Modeled with frictionless execution over the 182-day horizon.

Parity Expectation: Covered Interest Rate Parity holds; forward rates are derived theoretically from spot rates and interest differentials unless overridden by live market quotes.

Premium Treatment: Option premiums (PREM_PUT, PREM_CALL) are treated as upfront cash outflows settled on the trade date.
